# Introduction

Correlations with History, Today’s World, and Future Trends Dolores Cannon (1931–2014) was a hypnotherapist and author known for exploring metaphysical subjects via past-life regression. Over dozens of books – from Conversations with Nostradamus to The Convoluted Universe series and Between Death and Life – she claimed to uncover hidden knowledge, future prophecies, and insights on life, death, and cosmic existence 1 2 . This report examines Cannon’s major themes and predictions and compares them with documented historical events (past), current world developments (present), and emerging trends (near future). We analyze her material on hidden knowledge, religious and institutional transformation, global upheavals (war, disease, environmental change), shifts in human consciousness and “New Earth” concepts, and references to extraterrestrial contact and soul journeys. Each theme is cross-referenced with real-world data and research to identify patterns, correlations, and potential credibility. We highlight which elements of her work have already come true, which are unfolding now, and which may be on the horizon – noting insights that perhaps should not be ignored.
