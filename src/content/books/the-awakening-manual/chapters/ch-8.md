🧭 The 3 Core Layers of the Energetic You
1. The Physical Body

Your vessel. The temple. The interface through which energy is translated into action, feeling,
and sensation.

      Pro tip: The more hydrated and aligned it is, the more smoothly energy flows. Treat
      it like a luxury vehicle—water, movement, stretch, rest, nourish.

2. The Subtle Body

This is where your chakras, meridians, and aura come in. Think of this as the wiring system
behind your perception, emotion, intuition, and flow.

3. The Lightbody

Your highest energetic expression. Exists beyond linear time. Often activated during deep
meditations, dreams, or spontaneous spiritual flexes. This is the part of you that remembers.
