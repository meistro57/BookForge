# **Chapter 9**

**It’s Not Broken — It’s Broadband**

If you’ve lived with ADHD long enough, you’ve probably internalized a quiet, corrosive message:  
 “You’re too much. Too messy. Too scattered. Too intense.”

It’s the story society repeats until it seeps into our bones. But here’s the truth they never told you: **you’re not broken — you’re broadband.**

---

### **The Old Story**

The old narrative says ADHD is a flaw. A disorder. A lack.

* You can’t sit still.

* You miss deadlines.

* You get distracted by a shiny object in the middle of a serious meeting.

And yes, those challenges are real. But reducing your whole identity to that list is like judging a supercomputer because Solitaire doesn’t run smoothly on it.

---

### **The Reframe**

You’re not a glitch. You’re running a **different operating system.**

* Where others process one thing at a time, you process *everything*.

* Where others see a to-do list, you see ten possible futures branching at once.

* Where others hear noise, you pick up frequencies no one else notices.

It’s not a bug — it’s bandwidth.

---

### **Metaphors That Matter**

ADHD isn’t about being “less capable.” It’s about being wired for:

* **Rapid-fire pattern recognition.** You connect dots others don’t even see.

* **Hyperfocus flow states.** Hours disappear when the work lights you up.

* **Cosmic curiosity.** You ask “why” until the veil lifts.

Think of it like this: you’re not driving the wrong car. You’re flying the wrong vehicle in the wrong lane. A jet isn’t defective because it struggles in city traffic.

---

### **The Volunteer Idea**

Dolores Cannon — a past-life regression researcher — spoke about certain souls as **“volunteers,”** beings who incarnated to help Earth evolve in times of crisis. Whether you vibe with that or not, the metaphor fits:

ADHDers aren’t accidents. We’re not evolutionary leftovers. We’re upgrades. We’re the volunteers who showed up with extra antennae and a restless drive to ask, “What if?”

---

### **The Hack: Sparks vs. Fire Alarms**

One of the hardest parts of ADHD is emotional regulation. We feel big, fast, and fierce. But here’s a simple hack:

When a wave hits, ask yourself:

* Is this a **spark** (creative energy wanting expression)?

* Or a **fire alarm** (danger, stress, threat)?

Just labeling the difference buys you 10 seconds of grace. Ten seconds is often the difference between a meltdown and a breakthrough.

---

### **The Bigger Picture**

Yes, ADHD can be exhausting. But it’s also why some of the world’s greatest innovators, artists, and explorers were wired like us. We are restless because we are here to **move things forward.**

When you reframe ADHD as bandwidth, you stop seeing yourself as broken hardware and start seeing yourself as advanced software that just wasn’t given the right manual.

---

### **Closing Punchline**

We are not defective.  
 We are not a mistake.  
 **We are disruptive by design.**
