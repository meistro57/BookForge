# Chapter 2: Dolores Cannon and the Past Life Regression Revelations

### **Introduction: A Journey into the Unknown**

Dolores Cannon was a trailblazer in the field of past-life regression and hypnosis. Over the course of her 40-year career, she developed the Quantum Healing Hypnosis Technique (QHHT), which allowed her clients to access deep subconscious memories. Through thousands of hypnosis sessions, her subjects recounted past lives, extraterrestrial encounters, and memories of lost civilizations such as Atlantis and Lemuria.

Her work challenges conventional scientific and historical paradigms, suggesting that our past is not as simple as mainstream narratives claim. Instead, it points to a vast, interconnected history of reincarnation, multidimensional existence, and interactions with higher-dimensional beings. This chapter explores her discoveries, how they align with alternative historical theories, and what they reveal about humanity’s origins and purpose.

---

### **The Quantum Healing Hypnosis Technique (QHHT) and Past-Life Regression**

Dolores Cannon’s journey into past-life regression began in the 1960s when she and her husband, Johnny Cannon, started experimenting with hypnosis. What started as a simple therapeutic practice soon turned into a gateway to extraordinary revelations. QHHT differs from conventional hypnosis by accessing what she called the **Subconscious (or Higher Self)**—a vast source of knowledge that holds the soul’s past-life memories.

Through QHHT, her clients recalled:

* **Past lives in ancient and forgotten civilizations**, including Atlantis, Lemuria, and even pre-human epochs.  
* **Existences on other planets and star systems**, revealing a broader cosmic history where souls incarnate across dimensions.  
* **Memories of extraterrestrial influence on Earth**, including interactions with advanced beings who guided humanity’s development.

These accounts suggest that human history is far older and more intricate than mainstream academia acknowledges.

---

### **Lost Civilizations and the Cycle of Human Evolution**

Many of Cannon’s clients spoke of lives in technologically and spiritually advanced civilizations that predated our known history. These stories resonate with ancient texts and alternative archaeology, reinforcing the idea of cyclical human advancement and destruction.

Some of the key lost civilizations revealed in her sessions include:

* **Atlantis:** A highly advanced society that mastered energy manipulation, crystal technology, and spiritual wisdom. Many of her subjects described its eventual downfall due to corruption and misuse of power, aligning with Plato’s account.  
* **Lemuria (Mu):** An earlier civilization that focused on harmony with nature and multidimensional communication. Lemurians were said to be deeply connected to the Earth’s energies and possessed telepathic abilities.  
* **Prehistoric Civilizations:** Some regressions revealed lifetimes in societies that existed long before recorded history, suggesting humanity’s origins stretch back hundreds of thousands of years, possibly seeded by extraterrestrials.

These findings mirror the work of Graham Hancock and other alternative historians who propose that an advanced civilization was wiped out by a global catastrophe around 12,800 years ago.

---

### **Extraterrestrial Influences and Starseed Souls**

One of the most controversial aspects of Cannon’s work is her exploration of extraterrestrial influence on human evolution. Many of her clients claimed that they were **Starseeds**, souls that originated from other planets or dimensions and incarnated on Earth to assist with humanity’s spiritual evolution.

Common themes in these regressions include:

* **Intergalactic Councils & Galactic Federations:** Advanced extraterrestrial beings working together to guide developing civilizations.  
* **DNA Manipulation & Hybridization:** Some beings were described as influencing human genetics to accelerate spiritual and mental development.  
* **The Watchers:** Entities who observe human progress, ensuring that Earth follows its intended evolutionary path.

These narratives align with myths of sky gods and divine beings found in Sumerian, Hindu, and indigenous traditions worldwide. They also connect with modern theories about ancient astronauts and advanced beings intervening in human affairs.

---

### **Reincarnation, Karma, and the Soul’s Journey**

At the core of Cannon’s findings is the idea that human existence is **not confined to one lifetime**. Her subjects consistently described reincarnation as a soul’s journey through various experiences for growth and enlightenment. This is deeply embedded in Eastern traditions like Hinduism and Buddhism, as well as esoteric teachings in the West.

Key aspects of reincarnation explored through QHHT:

* **Karmic Balancing:** The concept that souls return to resolve unfinished lessons, often incarnating in different cultures, time periods, or even species.  
* **Parallel Lives and Timelines:** Some clients reported experiencing multiple lifetimes simultaneously, suggesting a multidimensional existence where time is non-linear.  
* **Soul Contracts & Life Planning:** Before incarnating, souls choose specific challenges, relationships, and experiences to accelerate their growth.

These ideas strongly parallel the teachings of the Seth material, the Law of One, and indigenous spiritual traditions that speak of an interconnected soul journey.

---

### **The New Earth and Humanity’s Evolution**

A recurring theme in Cannon’s later work was the transition of Earth into a higher vibrational state, a concept she termed **The New Earth**. According to her clients, humanity is undergoing a spiritual shift where individuals who raise their consciousness will move into a more harmonious and enlightened reality.

Key elements of the New Earth shift:

* **Separation of Densities:** Souls who are spiritually evolved will ascend into a higher-dimensional Earth, while others may reincarnate in different planetary systems suited to their vibration.  
* **Increased Extraterrestrial Contact:** As humanity progresses, contact with benevolent extraterrestrial beings will become more widespread.  
* **Activating Dormant Abilities:** Telepathy, heightened intuition, and self-healing abilities will emerge as humans remember their true nature.

This vision of an evolutionary leap mirrors the messages found in the Law of One, Kryon’s teachings, and various indigenous prophecies about the coming of a new age.

---

### **Conclusion: The Rediscovery of Ancient Wisdom**

Dolores Cannon’s work presents a fascinating intersection of past-life memories, lost civilizations, and extraterrestrial influences. Her findings challenge the mainstream historical narrative and offer profound insights into humanity’s origins and purpose. By examining reincarnation, lost civilizations, and interstellar connections, her research aligns with ancient teachings and alternative historical theories, painting a more expansive picture of our collective journey.

As we continue to uncover new archaeological discoveries and integrate spiritual wisdom, we may find that the truths hidden in past-life regressions are not merely personal experiences but fragments of a larger, forgotten history.

In the next chapter, we will explore **The Seth Material**, another groundbreaking source that reveals the nature of consciousness, reality creation, and the multidimensional nature of existence.
