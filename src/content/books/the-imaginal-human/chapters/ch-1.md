# 📖 Chapter One

## The Lie We’ve Been Told

*Or: How They Trained You to Forget You're a Creator by Weaponizing Santa Claus*

Let’s get one thing straight.

You weren’t born broken.  
 You weren’t born confused.  
 You were born **plugged in**—with a limitless, vibrant imagination, fully connected to your higher self, and capable of manifesting entire realities out of daydreams.

So what happened?

You were *taught* not to believe in it.

---

It started with stories. Innocent enough, right?

🧑‍🎄 **Santa Claus**  
 🐰 **The Easter Bunny**  
 🦷 **The Tooth Fairy**

They told you: *"Believe in the magic. He sees you when you’re sleeping\!"*  
 So you did.  
 You believed hard. You imagined it vividly.  
 You *felt* the magic in your bones.

And then—**boom**.  
 One day, they looked you in the eye and said:

> “It’s not real. You’re too old for that now.”  
>  “Time to grow up.”  
>  “Stop pretending.”

Let’s pause.

They built an entire foundation of magical thinking, encouraged you to trust it, feel it, use it—  
 *only to yank it away and laugh at you for believing it.*

---

### **🧠 The Damage Was Psychological Warfare**

That moment—when you found out Santa wasn’t real?  
 That wasn’t just disappointment. That was **programming.**  
 A calculated emotional betrayal meant to do one thing:

> Make you doubt your own inner world.

And it worked.

That inner sparkle you used to trust?  
 Now it came with hesitation. With shame. With “don’t be stupid.”  
 They mocked what you used to believe in, and you learned the unspoken rule:

> *“The things I feel deeply must not be real. Better to shut up and play along.”*

---

### **🛠 And From There, the Programming Got Sophisticated**

In school, you were praised for memorization, not imagination.  
 Standardized tests didn’t care about your vision—only your compliance.  
 Art class was “fun,” but not serious.  
 Daydreaming in math? Detention.

You were conditioned to *minimize the most sacred part of yourself*.

The part that:

* Constructs inner worlds with precision

* Visualizes outcomes and shifts timelines

* Receives intuitive downloads

* Interfaces with your subconscious and higher self  
   —was quietly filed under “nonsense.”

---

### **🤡 So Let’s Call It What It Is**

They made you believe in a magical man who rewards good thoughts—  
 then ridiculed you for having good thoughts.  
 They programmed you to associate belief with humiliation.  
 And they pathologized the very skillset that ancient mystics, healers, and multidimensional beings *used as standard operating protocol*.

Let’s be brutally honest:

> **They didn’t kill your imagination.**  
>  **They shamed it out of you.**  
>  And then sold you their own version back—via screens, subscriptions, and controlled narratives.

---

### **🔥 But Here's the Plot Twist:**

Your imagination never died.  
 It waited.

Behind every daydream you pushed aside...  
 Behind every vision you called “just silly”...  
 Behind every fantasy you almost followed until someone laughed...

It. Waited.

And now? It’s waking up.

---

This book isn’t here to gently remind you.  
 It’s here to **resurrect** the part of you that was never broken—just betrayed.  
 The part that used to create entire realities in your head before breakfast.  
 The part that can sculpt probability with a single, focused thought.

> **You are not broken.**  
>  **You are not crazy.**  
>  **You are a creator who was told to play dumb.**  
>  **But the game’s over.**

---

### **🎯 Action Prompt: *“Reclaim the Magic”***

1. Write down the first time you remember feeling betrayed for believing in something magical—Santa, the Easter Bunny, anything.

2. Under it, write this out loud:

> *“I wasn’t wrong to believe in magic. I was lied to about what magic really is.”*

Then imagine: what if you were right all along?
