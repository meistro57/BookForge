### **😈 Let’s Talk Fear Tactics**

The system has relied on a few key strategies to shut down the imagination:

* **Mockery**:  
   “That’s just in your head.”  
   “You sound crazy.”  
   “Time to grow up.”  
   ← This trains you to self-censor.

* **Misdirection**:  
   Feeding your imagination junk food—violent media, dystopian fantasies, trauma loops disguised as entertainment.

* **Pathologizing**:  
   “Imaginative” kids get labeled ADHD, delusional, or dissociative.  
   “Too creative” adults get medicated or ignored.

* **Shame**:  
   Being caught talking to yourself, drawing your dreams, crying over a vision—these are treated like *embarrassments,* not soul expressions.

All of this is designed to install a message:

> **“Don’t trust what you see inside. It’s not real.”**

Which is the biggest lie ever told.
