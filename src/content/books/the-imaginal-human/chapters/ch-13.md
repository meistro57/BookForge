#### **🔹 Step 1: Pick the Target**

Choose one area—pain, emotion, tension, imbalance.

#### **🔹 Step 2: Create the Scene**

Visualize the area in symbolic form:

* A glowing orb

* A cluster of tangled roots

* A dark mass

* A stormy sky

There are no wrong images. Whatever appears is *how your subconscious represents the issue.*

#### **🔹 Step 3: Transform It**

Now shift it.

* Light fills the area

* Roots untangle

* The storm clears

* A beam of golden energy dissolves the mass

Don’t just “see” it—*feel* it.  
 Imagine what it would be like to feel the healing as *complete.*

#### **🔹 Step 4: Seal the Frequency**

End the session by visualizing the area whole, strong, glowing, and at peace.  
 Say aloud:

> “My body remembers this frequency. I anchor it now.”
